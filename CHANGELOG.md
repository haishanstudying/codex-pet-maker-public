- A checksum-pinned synthetic `Shape Scout` effect showcase.

  result.

### Measured

- Reduced conservative orchestration-context replay from 109,515 to 11,964
  tokens (89.08%) on the 13-job synthetic workload.
- Kept the largest measured controller action at 872 bytes.

## [0.1.0+codex.local-20260727-r1] - 2026-07-28

### Added

- Resumable `make-codex-pet` workflow driven by a compact persistent controller.
- Deterministic atlas assembly, transparency cleanup, animation previews, and
  structural validation.
- Four-cardinal direction acceptance checks and bounded stage-specific retries.
- Source allowlisting and privacy scanning for review and publication.
- Synthetic geometry tests that contain no personal reference material.

### Privacy

- Kept references, prompts, generated images, QA output, and final packages
  outside the Plugin source and installation directory.
- Prohibited cross-run reuse of user inputs and generated assets.
